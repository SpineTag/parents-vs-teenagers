      Common Ground Tools

      Simple, real-world strategies that turn tense moments into collaborative conversations.

      Conversation script

      Reduce blame and keep the focus on solutions:

      1. I feel... name the emotion
      2. When... describe the situation, not the person
      3. I need... explain what would help
      4. Can we...? suggest a fair next step

      Example: "I feel stressed when homework is brought up right after school. I need a short break. Can we talk about it after 30 minutes?"

      Negotiation steps

      Move from positions to shared solutions:

      • Define the issue: Focus on one topic at a time.
      • Share both perspectives: Each person explains concerns and goals.
      • Identify non-negotiables: Name safety or core responsibilities clearly.
      • Brainstorm options: Look for compromises or trial periods.
      • Agree and review: Decide what to try and when to check in.

      Quick de-escalation

      When emotions rise, slow the moment down:

      • Take a short break if voices rise; return when calm.
      • Listen to understand before responding.
      • Avoid labels like "always" and "never."
      • Acknowledge the other person's concern, even if you disagree.
      • Focus on the current issue instead of past arguments.
